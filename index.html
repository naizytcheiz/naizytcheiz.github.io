Personal studio for short gaming clips

Kadr is a private tool used by a single creator. It cuts the creator's long gaming stream recordings into short vertical clips with subtitles, and publishes them to the creator's own Instagram, YouTube and TikTok accounts on a schedule the creator chooses.

How it works
  1. The creator adds a long video and Kadr finds the strongest moments.
  2. The creator reviews, edits and exports the clips.
  3. The creator connects their own social accounts through the official sign-in of each platform and schedules clips for publishing.

Kadr is not a public service: there is no sign-up, and it never accesses other users' accounts or data.
