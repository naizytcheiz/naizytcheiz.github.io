Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

Kadr is a personal video editing studio operated by a single owner on the owner's own computer. It is not offered to the public.

Data we process

Videos, transcripts and clips are stored locally on the owner's computer and deleted when a project expires.

When the owner connects Instagram, YouTube or TikTok through each platform's official sign-in, Kadr receives access tokens for the owner's own account. Kadr uses them only to read the owner's basic profile (username or channel name, TikTok scope user.info.basic) and to upload clips the owner has explicitly scheduled to the owner's own account (TikTok scopes video.publish and video.upload).

Storage and sharing

Access tokens are stored only on the owner's computer, are never sold or shared with third parties, and are deleted when the owner disconnects the account in Kadr. Kadr does not collect personal data of any other users.
